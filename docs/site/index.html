Prediction

Forecasts that show their confidence

A weighted moving average leans on your most recent cycles and tells you how sure it is — plain language, not a false-precision percentage.

  • Next period, fertile window and ovulation

  • Toggle fertile & ovulation overlays
  • Per-day detail sheet

Why it's different

Your health data never leaves your phone

Most trackers are data businesses wearing a health app. CycleCare is built the other way around: there is no account wall, no analytics SDK, and no ad network. Signing in is optional and gates nothing.

Local-first storage

Every entry lives on your device. The app makes no network calls in normal use.

App lock

Optional PIN, hashed into secure storage, plus biometric unlock.

No trackers

Zero analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs. Nothing profiles you.

Export & delete

Take everything with you as JSON, or erase all of it, in one tap from Settings.

How it works
